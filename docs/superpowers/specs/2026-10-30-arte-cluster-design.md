# IndustriesVerified · Arte — design

Date: 2026-10-30 · Owner: Dante Arola (first user) · Budget: ~20 h of build time

## 1. Intent

IndustriesVerified maps industries as living graphs (Green Panorama, BlockchainRio). Long term, the site is a set of
industry clusters that interconnect naturally. **Arte** is the next cluster, and it is the first one built around a
person instead of an event: a **career tool for emerging visual artists**.

The why, in Dante's words and experience: an emerging artist finds calls by chance (he found Liminales Expandidas on
Instagram), then loses days assembling the application (bio, motivation letter, 10 images with technical sheets, PDF
design). He wants a system that (1) understands his trajectory, (2) helps him apply and plans which calls are worth it,
(3) helps him discover artists whose work he may like (seeded from who he follows on Instagram + affinity of philosophy
and work), (4) gives him tools to grow, and (5) alerts him about exhibitions and calls. **Artists hate paperwork and
tracking; the product takes over the administrative life of the artist.**

Success: Dante uses it for real during and after Liminales (Nov 2026): his profile is complete, he applies to the open
calls that fit him (e.g. Studio Voltaire residency, Bienal Sur, FNA) with dossiers generated by the tool, and he never
misses a deadline. It must feel premium: calm, beautiful, trustworthy, fast.

## 3. Goals

1. **Onboarding that understands you (5 minutes).** Sign in, paste website + Instagram (optionally a CV PDF). The
   system imports what it can (IG profile + recent posts, site text and images, any existing node in our graph with
   its trajectory edges) and drafts a structured profile for the artist to confirm.
2. **Artist profile = single source of truth.** Works (images + technical sheet: title, year, technique, dimensions,
   series, text), CV entries (education, solo/group shows, prizes/grants, residencies, collections, press), bios
   (short/long, ES/EN), statement, application data (birth, residence, contact). Entered once, reused everywhere.
3. **Opportunity radar with judgement.** All programs, each with eligibility, requirements (what the application
   asks for), deadline, benefit, source and "verified on" date. Per artist: hard eligibility filter + fit score +
   strategic value + effort, with a plain-language "why" for every recommendation.
4. **Season plan.** A calendar of recommended applications sequenced by deadline and preparation time, with a monthly
   effort budget the artist sets ("2 applications a month").
5. **Applications pipeline.** Each application has a status (idea → preparing → sent → result), an auto-generated
   checklist from the call's requirements, due dates, files, notes; results flow back into the CV.
6. **Dossier generator.** From profile + call requirements: motivation letter draft in the artist's voice, suggested
   work selection, adapted bio length, formatted CV, rendered to a beautiful PDF that respects the call's limits
   (pages, MB, image count, file naming). Always editable; never sent without review.
7. **Discovery.** Affine artists (seed: Instagram following via HikerAPI, intersected with the graph; plus similarity of
   statements/themes/medium and graph proximity), affine galleries/curators, and "who walked this path": similar
   artists who got a given opportunity.
8. **Today / alerts.** A home screen: deadlines this week, new matching calls, openings and shows this week, nudges.
   In-app alerts first; weekly email digest when an email provider is configured.
9. **Public portfolio page** per artist (`/arte/a/<slug>`), auto-updating from the profile: a link that can replace the
   PDF in many calls.
10. **Arte cluster in IndustriesVerified.** The art graph is browsable like other clusters (map + node panels), and
    the career tool lives under `/arte`.

## 4. Non-goals (this round)

- Payments/subscriptions, teams, multi-language beyond ES (EN texts are generated content, UI is Spanish).
- Social features between artists (messaging, follows inside the platform), marketplace or sales.
- Auto-submitting applications on third-party sites or emailing curators on the artist's behalf.
- Native mobile apps (responsive web only).
- Rebuilding the Python ETL as TypeScript; it remains the offline data pipeline.

## 5. Anti-goals (failure even if it "works")

- **Invented facts.** Any call, date, requirement or relation shown must come from a source, with a link and a
  "verified on" date. Unknown is shown as unknown ("fecha a confirmar"), never guessed.
- **Generic AI letters.** Drafts must sound like the artist (learned from their own texts) and cite their real works;
  the UI makes editing the default path. No "As an emerging artist passionate about…" slop.
- **Notification spam** or anxiety-inducing UX. Calm, prioritized, few alerts.
- **Exposing private data.** Only public data from the graph is shared; the artist's profile, files and applications
  are private unless explicitly published to the portfolio.
- **Spam outreach** or anything that would burn the artist's reputation with galleries/curators.

## 6. Experience (for Dante, day one)

1. `/arte` landing: what it does, sign in (email magic link).
2. Onboarding: "Pegá tu web e Instagram" → import progress (IG profile, posts, site pages, graph match) → review screen
   with drafted bio, statement, CV entries and works (each editable; confirm/discard). For Dante, his /artme works and
   ¿SOS VOS? import as works; Liminales/Acéfala appears as a CV entry from the graph.
3. **Hoy** (home): next deadlines, top 3 recommended calls with "por qué", alerts, shows this week, profile completeness.
4. **Oportunidades**: list + filters (abiertas/próximas, tipo, país, digital, elegibles para mí), each card with fit
   score, why, deadline countdown, benefit, effort, "Postularme" → creates an application.
5. **Plan**: season calendar (12 months) with suggested applications and preparation windows.
6. **Postulaciones**: kanban + detail (checklist, dossier, files, notes, result).
7. **Dossier** editor: sections (letter, bio, CV, works), regenerate per section, live PDF preview, export.
8. **Perfil**: works manager (drag order, technical sheets), CV, bios, statement, application data, portfolio toggle.
9. **Descubrir**: affine artists/galleries/curators with reasons; IG-following seed.
10. **Mapa**: the art cluster graph (reusing the existing graph components) with the artist's own neighborhood highlighted.

Visual direction (my call): IndustriesVerified's dark base, but calmer and editorial for artists: generous spacing,
serif display headings, muted palette with one accent, real artwork imagery as the hero of each screen.

## 7. Architecture (my calls unless noted)

- **Same repo, same app** (`frontend/`), new routes under `/arte` (app router). Shared Supabase.
- **Database: Supabase Postgres.** Graph data goes into the existing `nodes`/`edges` tables tagged `industries:['arte']`
  (that is how clusters interconnect). Career data goes into new `arte_*` tables with Row Level Security by `auth.uid()`:
  `arte_profiles`, `arte_works`, `arte_cv_items`, `arte_texts` (bios/statement/letters with versions),
  `arte_programs` (opportunities, public read), `arte_program_requirements`, `arte_applications`,
  `arte_application_items` (checklist), `arte_alerts`, `arte_follows` (IG following seed), `arte_events` (shows/openings).
- **Auth:** Supabase Auth, email magic link.
- **Storage:** Supabase Storage. A private bucket for work images and files; public URLs only for works published to
  the portfolio.
- **Development:** Supabase local (Docker + CLI are installed). **Production needs a decision from Dante:** the current
  Green Panorama Supabase lives in an account this session can't reach, so either he adds the migrations there or
  creates a new project.
- **LLM:** reuse the repo's OpenRouter pattern (`OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, model in `ARTE_LLM_MODEL`). Every AI feature has
  a non-AI fallback (templates, rule-based scoring) so the app works without a key.
- **Instagram:** HikerAPI server-side (`HIKERAPI_KEY`), cached in the DB; used at onboarding, for following-seed and
  periodic refresh.
- **PDF:** `@react-pdf/renderer` server-side (serverless-friendly); images downscaled with `sharp` to meet MB limits.
- **Data sync:** a script that loads escena-arte (SQLite) into Supabase (graph + programs + events), idempotent upserts.
  Refresh cadence for programs: re-run the Python pipeline + sync (manual now; cron later).
- **Matching** (pure TypeScript, unit-tested): eligibility rules (nationality/residence/age/discipline/fee/deadline) →
  fit (medium, digital, themes, career stage) → strategic value (prestige tier, alumni similarity, benefit) → effort
  (requirements size vs. profile completeness). Output: score + reasons.
- **Tests:** Vitest for matching, eligibility parsing, dossier assembly, import mappers; Playwright smoke for
  sign-in → onboarding → application → dossier PDF.

## 8. Data model essentials

- `arte_programs`: id, name, type, organizer, country, url, description, benefit, eligibility (structured json:
  nationality, residence, age_min/max, disciplines, career_stage), requirements (json: letter, cv, images_n, pdf_pages_max,
  pdf_mb_max, video, budget, fee), opens_at, closes_at, status, recurrence/window, digital_focus, source, verified_at,
  alumni (node ids). Built from `programas` + LLM-assisted extraction of eligibility/requirements from the call text,
  with every extracted field kept alongside its source sentence.
- `arte_profiles`: user_id, node_id (link to graph artist), name, slug, birth_year, birth_place, base_city, website,
  instagram, mediums, themes, career_stage, monthly_application_budget, portfolio_public.
- `arte_works`: profile, title, year, technique, dimensions, series, description, images[], order, public.
- `arte_cv_items`: profile, kind (education/solo/group/prize/grant/residency/collection/press), title, venue/org,
  city/country, year, link, source (import/graph/manual), confirmed.
- `arte_applications`: profile, program, status, due_at, checklist, dossier (json + pdf path), notes, result, result_at.

Component library choices within Tailwind 4, exact visual system, copywriting, scoring weights (tunable),

### Builder-check answers (settled as my calls)

- **IG following → graph:** fetch the artist's following list (HikerAPI `/gql/user/following/chunk`, paginated, cached in
  `arte_follows`), match handles against `nodes.contact_info.instagram`; matched artists/venues seed Discovery, the rest
  are kept as raw handles for later enrichment.
- **Shows/openings data:** `arte_events` is filled by the sync from escena-arte (venue IG posts classified as
  openings/exhibitions with dates, Meridiano agenda); shown as "this week" + filtered by the artist's affine venues.
- **Seeding Dante (user zero):** a script maps `escena-arte/obras.json` (21 works with technical/philosophical texts and
  /artme image paths) into his works, his Liminales/Acéfala participation into CV, and links his profile to his graph node.
- **Requirements/eligibility extraction:** for each program, an LLM pass over the call text returns structured fields
  plus the quoted sentence that justifies each; without a key, regex extraction fills what it can and leaves the rest
  "a confirmar".
- **Career stage** (emerging / mid / established) is inferred from CV size and venue tiers, editable by the artist.
- **Magic links in local dev** go to Supabase's local mail catcher (Inbucket); production uses Supabase SMTP or a
  configured provider.
- **Image rights:** images imported from the artist's own site/IG are stored privately for their own use; graph photos
  of other artists are only linked (never re-published) outside the artist's private discovery view.
